Enterprise E-Commerce Platform - System Architecture Meeting
Date: May 8, 2026
Attendees: Product Team, Engineering Team, Business Analysts

=== MEETING TRANSCRIPT ===

Sarah (Product Manager): Good morning everyone. Today we're defining the architecture for our new enterprise e-commerce platform. Let me start with the high-level requirements.

We need a comprehensive system that handles the entire customer journey - from browsing products to post-purchase support. The platform must support multiple business models: B2C, B2B, and marketplace operations.

John (Lead Architect): Let's break this down systematically. Starting with the core entities:

CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT:
Every customer has a unique account with email, password, and profile information. Customers can be individuals or businesses. Business customers have additional attributes like company name, tax ID, and credit terms.

Each customer can have multiple addresses - shipping addresses and billing addresses. Addresses include street, city, state, postal code, and country. We need to support address validation and geocoding.

Customers have a wishlist where they can save products they're interested in. They can also follow specific brands or categories to get notifications.

Mike (Database Architect): For authentication and security, we need to implement:
- Multi-factor authentication with SMS and email verification
- OAuth integration for social login (Google, Facebook, Apple)
- Session management with JWT tokens
- Password reset functionality with secure token generation
- Account lockout after failed login attempts
- Activity logging for security audits

PRODUCT CATALOG:
Products are the core of our system. Each product has:
- SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) - unique identifier
- Name, description, and detailed specifications
- Brand association
- Category hierarchy (products can belong to multiple categories)
- Pricing information including base price, sale price, and discount rules
- Tax classification
- Inventory tracking across multiple warehouses
- Product variants (size, color, material, etc.)
- Product images and videos
- SEO metadata (title, description, keywords)
- Product reviews and ratings
- Related products and cross-sell recommendations

Categories form a hierarchical tree structure. For example:
Electronics > Computers > Laptops > Gaming Laptops

Each category has attributes, filters, and sorting options specific to that category.

Lisa (Business Analyst): For pricing, we need sophisticated rules:
- Base pricing per product
- Tiered pricing based on quantity (bulk discounts)
- Customer-specific pricing for B2B clients
- Promotional pricing with start and end dates
- Dynamic pricing based on demand and inventory
- Currency conversion for international sales
- Tax calculation based on customer location

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT:
We operate multiple warehouses across different regions. Each warehouse maintains inventory for products. We need real-time inventory tracking with:
- Current stock levels
- Reserved quantities (items in pending orders)
- Available to promise (ATP) calculations
- Reorder points and automatic purchase order generation
- Inventory transfers between warehouses
- Stock adjustments and cycle counting
- Lot and serial number tracking for certain products
- Expiration date tracking for perishable items

SHOPPING EXPERIENCE:
Customers browse products and add them to their shopping cart. The cart is persistent across sessions and devices. Cart items include:
- Product reference
- Selected variant
- Quantity
- Unit price at time of addition
- Applied discounts
- Estimated delivery date

Customers can save carts for later or share them with others. We need cart abandonment tracking and recovery emails.

CHECKOUT PROCESS:
During checkout, customers:
1. Review cart items
2. Select or add shipping address
3. Choose shipping method (standard, express, overnight)
4. Select or add payment method
5. Apply coupon codes or gift cards
6. Review order summary with all costs
7. Confirm and place order

We need to calculate:
- Subtotal (sum of item prices)
- Shipping costs based on weight, dimensions, and destination
- Taxes based on jurisdiction
- Discounts and promotions
- Gift card or store credit application
- Final total

ORDER MANAGEMENT:
Once an order is placed, it goes through several states:
- Pending: Order received, payment being processed
- Payment Confirmed: Payment successful
- Processing: Order being prepared
- Shipped: Order dispatched from warehouse
- In Transit: Order with carrier
- Out for Delivery: Final delivery stage
- Delivered: Successfully delivered
- Cancelled: Order cancelled by customer or system
- Returned: Customer initiated return
- Refunded: Refund processed

Each order contains:
- Order number (unique identifier)
- Customer reference
- Order date and time
- Billing address
- Shipping address
- Order items (products, quantities, prices)
- Subtotal, tax, shipping, discounts, total
- Payment information
- Shipping method
- Tracking information
- Order notes and special instructions

PAYMENT PROCESSING:
We need to support multiple payment methods:
- Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
- Digital wallets (PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- Bank transfers
- Buy Now Pay Later (Affirm, Klarna)
- Gift cards and store credit
- Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum)

Payment processing includes:
- Payment authorization
- Payment capture
- Refund processing
- Partial refunds
- Payment retry logic for failed transactions
- Fraud detection and prevention
- PCI compliance for card data
- Payment reconciliation

SHIPPING AND FULFILLMENT:
Integration with multiple carriers:
- FedEx, UPS, USPS, DHL
- Real-time rate calculation
- Label generation
- Tracking number assignment
- Delivery confirmation
- Signature requirements
- Insurance options

Fulfillment workflow:
1. Order received
2. Inventory allocated
3. Pick list generated
4. Items picked from warehouse
5. Quality check
6. Packing with packing slip
7. Shipping label applied
8. Carrier pickup
9. Tracking updates
10. Delivery confirmation

RETURNS AND REFUNDS:
Customers can initiate returns within 30 days. Return process:
- Customer requests return authorization (RMA)
- System generates return label
- Customer ships item back
- Warehouse receives and inspects item
- Refund processed or exchange initiated
- Inventory updated

Return reasons include:
- Wrong item received
- Defective product
- Changed mind
- Better price found
- Item not as described

CUSTOMER SUPPORT:
Support ticket system for customer inquiries:
- Ticket creation from multiple channels (email, chat, phone)
- Ticket assignment to support agents
- Priority levels (low, medium, high, urgent)
- Status tracking (open, in progress, waiting, resolved, closed)
- Internal notes and customer communications
- Escalation workflow
- SLA tracking
- Knowledge base integration

REVIEWS AND RATINGS:
Customers can review purchased products:
- Star rating (1-5)
- Written review
- Photos and videos
- Verified purchase badge
- Helpful votes from other customers
- Seller responses
- Review moderation and approval

NOTIFICATIONS:
Multi-channel notification system:
- Email notifications
- SMS notifications
- Push notifications (mobile app)
- In-app notifications

Notification types:
- Order confirmation
- Shipping updates
- Delivery confirmation
- Payment receipts
- Promotional offers
- Price drop alerts
- Back in stock alerts
- Review requests
- Cart abandonment reminders

ANALYTICS AND REPORTING:
Business intelligence requirements:
- Sales reports (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Product performance metrics
- Customer behavior analysis
- Conversion funnel tracking
- Inventory turnover reports
- Revenue forecasting
- Customer lifetime value
- Cohort analysis
- A/B test results

MARKETPLACE FEATURES:
For marketplace model, we need:
- Seller accounts and profiles
- Seller onboarding and verification
- Product listing management for sellers
- Commission calculation
- Seller payouts
- Seller performance metrics
- Dispute resolution
- Seller ratings and reviews

PROMOTIONS AND MARKETING:
Promotional engine supporting:
- Percentage discounts
- Fixed amount discounts
- Buy X Get Y offers
- Free shipping promotions
- Bundle deals
- Flash sales
- Coupon codes
- Loyalty points program
- Referral rewards
- Seasonal campaigns

SEARCH AND DISCOVERY:
Advanced search functionality:
- Full-text search across products
- Faceted search with filters
- Auto-complete suggestions
- Search result ranking
- Personalized recommendations
- Recently viewed items
- Trending products
- Similar product suggestions

MOBILE APPLICATION:
Native mobile apps (iOS and Android) with:
- Barcode scanning
- Push notifications
- Mobile wallet integration
- Location-based features
- Offline browsing
- Augmented reality product preview

ADMIN DASHBOARD:
Comprehensive admin interface for:
- Product management (CRUD operations)
- Order management and fulfillment
- Customer management
- Inventory management
- Promotion management
- Content management
- User role and permission management
- System configuration
- Report generation

INTEGRATIONS:
Third-party integrations required:
- Payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Square)
- Shipping carriers APIs
- Email service provider (SendGrid, Mailchimp)
- SMS gateway (Twilio)
- Analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel)
- CRM system (Salesforce)
- ERP system integration
- Accounting software (QuickBooks)
- Tax calculation service (Avalara)
- Fraud detection (Signifyd)

SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE:
- PCI DSS compliance for payment data
- GDPR compliance for EU customers
- CCPA compliance for California customers
- Data encryption at rest and in transit
- Regular security audits
- Penetration testing
- DDoS protection
- Rate limiting
- API authentication and authorization

PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS:
- Page load time < 2 seconds
- API response time < 200ms
- Support 10,000 concurrent users
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- Horizontal scalability
- CDN for static assets
- Database query optimization
- Caching strategy (Redis)

Sarah: This is comprehensive. Let's also consider the data relationships:

RELATIONSHIPS SUMMARY:
- Customer HAS MANY Addresses
- Customer HAS ONE Wishlist
- Wishlist CONTAINS MANY Products
- Customer PLACES MANY Orders
- Order BELONGS TO One Customer
- Order HAS MANY Order Items
- Order Item REFERENCES One Product
- Order HAS ONE Shipping Address
- Order HAS ONE Billing Address
- Order HAS MANY Payments
- Payment BELONGS TO One Order
- Order HAS MANY Shipments
- Shipment CONTAINS MANY Order Items
- Product BELONGS TO One Brand
- Product BELONGS TO MANY Categories
- Product HAS MANY Variants
- Product HAS MANY Reviews
- Review WRITTEN BY One Customer
- Product STORED IN MANY Warehouses (Inventory)
- Customer HAS MANY Support Tickets
- Support Ticket ASSIGNED TO One Agent
- Seller LISTS MANY Products (Marketplace)
- Promotion APPLIES TO MANY Products
- Customer EARNS MANY Loyalty Points
- Order GENERATES One Invoice

John: Perfect. This gives us a complete picture of the system. We'll need to design the database schema, API endpoints, and microservices architecture based on these requirements.

Mike: I'll start working on the entity-relationship diagram and database normalization.

Lisa: I'll document the business rules and validation requirements for each entity.

Sarah: Excellent. Let's reconvene next week to review the technical design. Meeting adjourned.
