E-Commerce Platform Requirements Meeting

Product Manager: Let's define our e-commerce system requirements.

CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT:
Customers have accounts with email, password, name, and phone number.
Each customer can have multiple shipping addresses and billing addresses.
Customers can create wishlists to save products they like.
Customers have a loyalty points balance that they earn from purchases.

PRODUCT CATALOG:
Products have SKU, name, description, price, and category.
Each product belongs to one brand.
Products can have multiple variants like size and color.
Products have inventory quantities tracked in warehouses.
Customers can write reviews for products with ratings and comments.

SHOPPING CART:
Customers add products to their shopping cart.
Cart items include product, quantity, and price.
Carts are saved across sessions.

ORDER MANAGEMENT:
Customers place orders from their cart.
Orders contain order items (product, quantity, price).
Orders have shipping address and billing address.
Orders go through states: pending, processing, shipped, delivered.
Each order has an order date and total amount.

PAYMENT:
Orders are paid using payment methods like credit card or PayPal.
Payments have status: pending, completed, or failed.
Payments store transaction ID and amount.

SHIPPING:
Orders are shipped by carriers like FedEx or UPS.
Shipments have tracking numbers.
Shipments contain order items being shipped.

RETURNS:
Customers can return orders within 30 days.
Returns have return reason and status.
Approved returns generate refunds.

PROMOTIONS:
Promotional codes give discounts on orders.
Promotions have discount percentage or fixed amount.
Promotions have start date and end date.

SUPPORT:
Customers create support tickets for issues.
Tickets are assigned to support agents.
Tickets have priority and status.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Customer has many addresses
- Customer has one wishlist
- Customer places many orders
- Order belongs to customer
- Order has many order items
- Order item references product
- Product belongs to brand
- Product belongs to category
- Product has many reviews
- Review written by customer
- Order has one payment
- Order has one shipment
- Shipment has tracking
- Customer has many support tickets
- Promotion applies to orders
