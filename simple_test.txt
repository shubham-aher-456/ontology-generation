Online Store System

We need a simple online store.

Customers can register with email and password.
Each customer has a name and phone number.

Products have a name, price, and description.
Products belong to categories like Electronics or Clothing.

Customers add products to their shopping cart.
The cart shows the total price.

Customers place orders from their cart.
Each order has an order date and status.
Order status can be pending, shipped, or delivered.

Orders are paid using credit card or PayPal.
Each payment has an amount and payment date.

Products are shipped to the customer address.
Each shipment has a tracking number.
